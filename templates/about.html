About Us

The Innovation Club at Meru National Polytechnic is a dynamic platform that empowers students to explore creativity, technology, and problem-solving through practical projects.

It brings together individuals with diverse skills to collaborate, share ideas, and develop innovative solutions that address real-world challenges.

The club organizes activities such as hackathons, workshops, and tech talks to enhance members’ knowledge and hands-on experience.

Through teamwork and mentorship, members are encouraged to grow their skills, build impactful projects, and prepare for future careers in technology and entrepreneurship.

Our Goal: To inspire innovation, creativity, and collaboration among students.
