Smart Aquaculture System

A Smart Aquaculture System is an automated technology solution designed to improve fish farming by using sensors, IoT devices, and data monitoring to manage water quality and fish health in real time. It continuously tracks key environmental factors such as water temperature, pH levels, dissolved oxygen, turbidity, and ammonia concentration. When any of these values go beyond safe limits, the system can trigger alerts or automatically activate corrective actions like water aeration, filtration, or feeding control.

Development of a PLC-Controlled Automatic Motor Coil Winding Machine

This project involves creating a precise automated system to replace manual coil winding. It uses a Programmable Logic Controller (PLC) to manage motor speed, winding turns, positioning and tension control to produce consistent high-quality coils for motors and transformers.

Automated Chick Hatch

An automated chick hatch system mimics a broody hen by controlling environmental conditions such as temperature, humidity, ventilation, and egg turning. It achieves high hatch success rates while reducing human intervention and improving poultry production efficiency.

Certificate Award

Members are awarded certificates after successfully completing training, innovation activities, technical projects and skills development programs. The awards recognize creativity, commitment, leadership and practical achievements.
